Built for small businesses

Big companies use expensive planning systems. Small food and beverage shops are left guessing, so they run out or throw expired stock away. CLaiR is a simple assistant that fixes that.

P

Predict

Forecasts demand from sales, holidays and seasons. Starts with Exponential Smoothing and moves to ARIMA as your history grows.

P

Prevent

Watches expiry dates and stock levels to avoid both shortages and overstock.

R

Recover

Points unavoidable waste to nearby recycling or waste-processing partners.
